## 1. NYSCA State of the Arts Fellowship, 2026–27

### Program and timing evidence
Source: https://arts.ny.gov/SOAfellow (Timeline and Program Overview).

- Open as checked: applications opened September 23, 2026; close October 21, 2026 at 5:00 p.m.
- Deadline timezone: UNKNOWN. The landing page does not specify it. Do not silently convert to UTC or label EST.
- Four individual artist placements; all artistic disciplines invited. Award: $60,000 per fellow inclusive of project expenses.
- Project geography: New York host-agency assignments, with locations varying by agency (statewide sites or Bronx, Albany, Manhattan, Staten Island). This is distinct from applicant residency.
- Fellowship begins February 2027; research precedes implementation; program ends spring 2028.
- Short excerpt: “Applications are submitted online.”

### Application evidence and guided steps
Official application: https://survey.alchemer.com/s3/8845598/NYSCA-Artist-Fellowship-Application-2026
Source: application questions 1–17, directly readable without submission.

1. Provide contact/address information, artistic discipline, and choose one host agency.
2. Write interest (300-word maximum), practice relevance (500), and artist statement (500). This requests inquiry, not a predetermined project proposal.
3. Upload résumé/CV PDF and work-sample PDF; supply two professional references with relationship and contact details.
4. Certify current New York State or in-state Tribal Nation residency. If advancing, provide two different current residency-document types; redact sensitive financial/identity details where specified.
5. Work samples: up to five captioned images; up to three audio/video URLs, each at most five minutes; literary samples at most four pages. URLs must be public/non-password-protected and remain live through December 31, 2026.

Fees and match: UNKNOWN, not specified in inspected application/landing page. Broader eligibility: PARTIAL verification only.

## 2. LMCC Creative Engagement, 2027 cycle

### Cycle, eligibility and geography
Official guidelines: https://lmcc.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Creative-Engagement-Program-Guidelines-2027.pdf (pages 2–7).

- Open: September 8–November 12, 2026; deadline 6 p.m. EST (explicit). Request range $1,000–$20,000.
- Project: public-facing completed artistic work in Manhattan during calendar 2027, with a confirmed creative contributor and professional artist fees budgeted.
- Recipient geography: generally Manhattan address/residency, separately from project location. A specifically documented Tribal Nation-member route has distinct NYSCA eligibility; do not erase it with a blanket Manhattan rule.
- Individuals: 18+, not full-time degree students; may represent a collective and may be sole proprietors, LLCs or S-Corps. This does NOT make it an unrestricted business grant.
- Nonprofits need an artistic mission/programming history. Funding-source rules differ: DCLA operating budget under $100,000; HGF under $250,000 and performing arts; NYSCA different direct-funding exclusions. Review source-specific matrix, not one generalized eligibility claim.
- First step is eligibility quiz; choose recipient carefully because it cannot change after submission.
- Required: one work sample; second optional. Income/expenses use portal table.
- Short excerpt: “Grant Application Deadline: Thursday, November 12, 2026, at 6 P.M. EST”.

### Required-document branches
Official application preview: https://lmcc.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2027-Creative-Engagement-Application-Questions.pdf (individual branch pages 12–13; nonprofit page 9).

- Individuals: one qualifying proof of physical Manhattan address, clearly dated within three months; no P.O. boxes. Allowed types include utility bill, redacted bank/card statement, driver’s license/IDNYC, current lease or voter registration.
- Nonprofits: most recent IRS 990, or itemized completed-year income/expenses if filing 990N; operating-expense answers; authorized signatory details. Conditional larger-budget branch requests projected organizational budget and board/staff list.
- Other recipient/fiscal-sponsor branches differ. Consult the applicable preview branch; do not ask every applicant for all documents.

### Application route and operational steps
Official program/FAQ: https://lmcc.net/resources/manhattan-arts-grants/creative-engagement/
Official portal index: https://lmcc.submittable.com/submit
Cycle-specific official target verified through its link/redirect: https://lmcc.submittable.com/submit/354150/creative-engagement-umez-arts-engagement-2027-application-form
The target redirects to Submittable login; authenticated form not inspected.

After eligibility/recipient review, create or sign into Submittable, complete applicable narrative/document fields, enter budget within the form, save drafts as needed, and submit by the deadline. FAQ says a confirmation email signals successful submission. Separate budget upload is not required. Applicant sessions are support resources, not established mandatory attendance. Only one proposal to Creative Engagement OR Creative Unrestricted per cycle. Shared Creative Engagement/UMEZ application does not mean identical eligibility.

Fees and matching funds: UNKNOWN; do not infer zero or no-match from silence. Artist compensation is a project budget requirement, not an application fee.
